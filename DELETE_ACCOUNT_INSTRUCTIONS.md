# Account Deletion Instructions

## How to Delete Your Outdoorda Account

We understand you may want to delete your account at any time. This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to permanently delete your account and associated data.

### Important Information Before Deleting Your Account

- **Permanent Action:** Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your profile information, service history, and communications will be deleted.
- **Outstanding Transactions:** Please ensure all pending payments and active service requests are completed or canceled before deletion.
- **Data Retention:** Some information may be retained for legal and tax compliance purposes as outlined in our Privacy Policy.
- **Rating and Reviews:** Ratings and reviews you've provided may remain visible to other users, but they will be anonymized.

---

## Method 1: Delete Account Through the App

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the Outdoorda App**
   - Launch the Outdoorda application on your device

2. **Navigate to Settings**
   - Tap the menu icon (three horizontal lines) or account icon
   - Select **Settings**

3. **Go to Account Settings**
   - Scroll down to find **Account** or **Profile Settings**
   - Tap on **Account Settings**

4. **Find the Delete Account Option**
   - Look for **Delete Account** or **Close Account**
   - This option is typically at the bottom of the Account Settings menu

5. **Start the Deletion Process**
   - Tap **Delete Account**
   - A warning message will appear reminding you of the permanent nature of this action

6. **Confirm Your Identity**
   - Enter your password or use biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition)
   - This is required to verify your identity for security purposes

7. **Provide Feedback (Optional)**
   - The app may ask for feedback on why you're deleting your account
   - You can optionally provide this information to help us improve our service

8. **Final Confirmation**
   - Review the deletion confirmation dialog
   - Tap **Confirm Deletion** or **Yes, Delete My Account**
   - You may need to confirm a second time to proceed

9. **Account Deletion Complete**
   - Your account has been successfully deleted
   - You will be logged out of the app
   - The app will return to the login screen

## Method 2: Delete Account Through Email

If you cannot delete your account through the app or prefer to request deletion via email:

### Steps

1. **Send an Email Request**
   - Email: **support@outdoorda.com**
   - Subject: **Account Deletion Request**

2. **Include the Following Information**
   - Your full name
   - Email address associated with your account
   - Your phone number (optional but helpful)
   - Reason for deletion (optional)
   - A clear statement: "I request permanent deletion of my Outdoorda account"

3. **Email Template**
   ```
   Subject: Account Deletion Request

   Dear Outdoorda Support Team,

   I would like to request the permanent deletion of my Outdoorda account.

   Account Information:
   - Full Name: [Your Full Name]
   - Email: [Your Email Address]
   - Phone: [Your Phone Number]
   - Reason: [Optional reason]

   I understand that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

   Thank you,
   [Your Name]
   ```

4. **Verification Process**
   - Our support team will verify your identity
   - You may be asked to confirm your request via email
   - Verification typically takes 24-48 hours

5. **Confirmation**
   - You will receive an email confirming the successful deletion of your account
   - Your data will be removed from our systems within the timeframe specified in our Privacy Policy

## What Happens After Account Deletion?

### Immediate Actions
- Your account becomes inaccessible
- You are logged out of the app
- Your profile is no longer visible to other users
- Active service requests are canceled

### Data Removal Timeline
- **Immediately:** Account access is terminated
- **Within 30 days:** Personal profile information is deleted from our main systems
- **Extended Period:** Some data may remain in backups for security and recovery purposes
- **Legal Retention:** Transaction records retained for 7 years per legal requirements

### What Remains
- Anonymized ratings and reviews you provided
- Anonymized transaction records (for legal and tax purposes)
- Aggregated analytics data (non-identifiable)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I recover my account after deletion?
**A:** No, account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All account data will be deleted. If you wish to use Outdoorda again, you will need to create a new account.

### Q: Will my messages be deleted?
**A:** Yes, all your messages and chat histories will be deleted. Other users will see messages from a deleted account as "User Deleted" or anonymous.

### Q: What about my payment information?
**A:** Your payment information is securely handled by our payment processor (Stripe) and will not be stored by us after account deletion. Stripe maintains transaction records for compliance purposes.

### Q: Will my ratings and reviews be deleted?
**A:** Your ratings and reviews will remain visible to maintain service quality and trust, but they will be anonymized and no longer linked to your account.

### Q: What if I have an active service request?
**A:** Please cancel or complete all active service requests before deleting your account. If you attempt to delete while services are active, you will be prompted to resolve them first.

### Q: How long does account deletion take?
**A:** Your account is immediately deactivated, but complete data removal may take up to 30 days.

### Q: Will I receive a confirmation email?
**A:** Yes, you will receive a confirmation email once your account deletion is processed.

### Q: What if I change my mind?
**A:** You cannot undo account deletion. However, you can create a new account and migrate your service history if needed.

### Q: Is my data secure during deletion?
**A:** Yes, all data is handled securely and deleted using industry-standard methods.

### Q: What about outstanding payments?
**A:** Please ensure all outstanding payments are settled before account deletion. Deletion does not cancel payment obligations.

## Data Deletion Under GDPR and CCPA

### GDPR (European Users)
If you are located in the EU, UK, or EEA, you have the right to erasure under GDPR Article 17. Your data will be deleted within 30 days of confirmation, with exceptions for legal obligations.

**Contact:** dpo@outdoorda.com

### CCPA (California Users)
If you are a California resident, you have the right to deletion under CCPA. Respond to deletion requests within 45 days.

**Contact:** privacy@outdoorda.com

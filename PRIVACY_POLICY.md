# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Outdoorda ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App"), including any other media form, media channel, mobile website, or mobile application related or connected to it.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Registration Information:** Name, email address, phone number, profile picture, and authentication credentials
- **Profile Information:** Biography, location, service expertise, business information, and verification documents
- **Service Information:** Job descriptions, service details, pricing information, and service preferences
- **Communication Data:** Messages, chat history, ratings, and reviews exchanged with other users
- **Payment Information:** Billing address, payment method details processed through our payment processor (Stripe)
- **Media Files:** Photos, documents, and other files you upload to the App
- **Support Communications:** Customer service inquiries, feedback, and support tickets

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, device settings, and mobile network information
- **Usage Information:** Features accessed, actions performed, time and duration of activities, search queries, clicks, pages or content viewed, and referring/exit pages
- **Location Information:** With your permission, precise or approximate location based on GPS, Bluetooth, and WiFi signals
- **Camera and File Access:** Photos and files accessed through device storage when you grant permission
- **Push Notifications:** Data related to push notification preferences and interactions
- **Analytics Data:** Information collected through Firebase Analytics about your interactions with the App

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Firebase Services:** Authentication data, crash reports, and analytics from Firebase
- **Payment Processor (Stripe):** Transaction information and payment verification
- **Social Media:** If you connect social media accounts, we may receive profile information
- **Other Users:** Service providers and customers may provide information about you, including ratings and reviews

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Account Management:** Creating and maintaining your account, verifying identity, and managing passwords
- **Service Delivery:** Processing service requests, job matching, and facilitating connections between service providers and customers
- **Communication:** Sending transactional emails, notifications, updates about services, and responding to inquiries
- **Payment Processing:** Processing payments, billing, and financial transactions through Stripe
- **Push Notifications:** Sending job updates, messages, and service notifications
- **Analytics:** Understanding user behavior, improving the App's functionality, and optimizing user experience
- **Ratings and Reviews:** Collecting and displaying user feedback and performance metrics
- **Support:** Providing customer service and technical support
- **Legal Compliance:** Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our Terms of Service
- **Fraud Prevention:** Detecting and preventing fraudulent activities and unauthorized access
- **Marketing:** Sending promotional content (with your consent) and improving marketing campaigns

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:
- **Firebase:** Authentication, analytics, cloud storage, and messaging
- **Stripe:** Payment processing and billing

### 4.2 Service Transactions

When you request or provide services, necessary information is shared:

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in good faith belief that such disclosure is necessary to:
- Comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
- Enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements
- Protect the safety, rights, and property of our users and the public
- Detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues

### 4.4 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, dissolution, reorganization, or similar transaction, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

### 4.5 With Your Consent

We may share your information with third parties with your explicit consent for specific purposes.

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

- Encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS)
- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Access controls and role-based permissions
- Regular security assessments and updates
- Secure data storage practices

**Important:** No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information:

- **Account Information:** Retained during your account tenure and for a reasonable period after deletion
- **Transaction Information:** Retained for legal and tax compliance purposes (minimum 7 years)
- **Communication Data:** Retained while conversations are relevant for service history
- **Analytics Data:** Generally retained for 12-24 months
- **Marketing Communications:** Retained until you unsubscribe

You may request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal retention requirements.

## 12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right to Know:** You may request what categories of personal information we collect, use, or sell
- **Right to Delete:** You may request deletion of personal information collected (with exceptions)
- **Right to Opt-Out:** You may opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

For CCPA requests, contact us using the information in Section 13.

## 13. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, United Kingdom, or European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right of Access:** Access your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion (right to be forgotten)
- **Right to Restriction:** Restrict processing of your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive data in a portable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** Contact your local data protection authority

Our lawful basis for processing includes your consent, contractual necessity, legal obligations, and legitimate interests. For GDPR-related inquiries, contact our Data Protection Officer using the information in Section 13.

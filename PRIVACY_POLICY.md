# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PD USA collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

- Account information: name, email, phone number, profile photo (if provided), and user role.
- Service data: pet details, service/job address details, notes, status, uploaded photos/files.
- Messaging data: in-app messages and attachments.
- Payments: payment status/metadata from payment providers. Full card numbers are not stored on our backend.
- Technical data: push notification token and basic device/app metadata for reliability and security.

## How We Use Information

- Provide account access and app features.
- Match customers, installers, and jobs.
- Enable bidding, scheduling, job tracking, and messaging.
- Send push notifications.
- Process payment-related workflows.
- Prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve service quality.

## Permissions Used

- Camera: capture profile/job/chat images.
- Photo Library: upload profile/job/chat images.
- Notifications: send job/account alerts.

You can manage permissions in device settings.

## Information Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the service:

- With other users involved in a job (customer/installer/admin).
- With service providers supporting messaging, storage, and payments.
- If required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information.

## Data Retention

We keep data as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

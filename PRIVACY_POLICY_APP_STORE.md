## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and certain information automatically when you use the App.

1.1 Information you provide directly

- Account details: name, email address, phone number, profile photo.
- Service request content: job descriptions, photos, property details, and other information you include when creating a request.
- Payment information: when you make payments we use third‑party payment processors (Stripe) to handle financial data — we do not store complete card numbers on our servers.
- Messages: text, images and attachments sent via in‑app messaging.
- Support requests: any information you provide when contacting support.

1.2 Information collected automatically

- Device information: device model, operating system version, app version, unique device identifiers.
- Usage information: pages/screens viewed, features used, session time, and crash logs (for diagnostics).
- Push notification tokens: required to send you notifications.
- Location: We may collect approximate location (city/region) if you choose to provide it to help match you with nearby installers. We do not collect precise continuous location unless you explicitly enable such a feature and consent to location collection.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to operate, provide, improve, and personalize the App, including to:

- Provide and manage service requests, quotes, messaging, and payments.
- Facilitate communications between customers, installers, and admins.
- Process payments through third‑party processors.
- Send transactional and promotional messages, subject to your preferences and applicable law.
- Improve and analyze our services, diagnose issues, and prevent fraud.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

## 3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information as described below:

- Service providers: with vendors who perform services for us (payment processors, analytics providers, cloud hosting, messaging/push services).
- Business partners: with installers and service providers necessary to fulfill a request.
- Legal reasons: when required by law, in response to legal process, or to protect rights, property, safety, or to prevent fraud.
- Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale, reorganization, or acquisition.

Where required, we use contracts and safeguards to protect personal information shared with service providers.

## 4. Third‑Party Services and SDKs

We use third‑party services to provide core functionality. These third parties may collect data independently under their own privacy policies. Notable examples used by the App:

- Firebase (analytics, messaging, crash reporting)
- Stripe (payment processing)

Please review the privacy policies of those providers for details. We link to them where appropriate in our App and in the app store listing.

## 5. Analytics & Advertising

We may use analytics tools to understand usage and improve the App. We do not use third‑party advertising SDKs to display targeted ads inside the App. If we later introduce advertising or tracking for advertising purposes, we will disclose it and provide opt‑out choices in accordance with applicable laws.

## 6. Push Notifications

The App uses push notifications for transactional updates (job status, new quotes, messages). You can opt out or manage notification preferences via your device settings.

## 7. Payments

Payments are processed by third‑party payment processors (for example, Stripe). We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers. For payment questions or disputes, contact sales@petdoorusa.com.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us to request deletion.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal information as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer required, we will delete or de‑identify it in accordance with our policies and applicable law.

## Description
Outdoorda connects customers, installers, and admins for seamless pet‑door service requests, quoting, messaging, and secure payments.

What it does:

- Request a service: describe your pet door job, attach photos, and submit a request.
- Compare quotes: receive competitive bids from local certified installers and pick the best.
- In‑app messaging: chat with installers to clarify details and share images before work begins.
- Secure payments: pay through the app with built‑in Stripe support and track invoices.
- Installer tools: manage requests, submit bids, message customers, and update job status.
- Admin controls: monitor jobs, manage users, and resolve disputes.

Why you’ll love it:

- Streamlined booking, honest quotes, transparent communication, and secure checkout make pet‑door installation simple and reliable.

Who it’s for:

- Homeowners, landlords, and professional installers who need a focused marketplace for pet‑door services.

Support & safety:

- Built‑in identity checks for professionals, photo‑based estimates to reduce surprises, and customer support built into the app.
